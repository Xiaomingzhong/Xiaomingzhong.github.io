Better feedback.

V0.3.1 removes the artificial confidence percentage. Users now get concrete guidance based on finger state, a longer stability check, and session statistics based on actual behavior instead of a synthetic score.

10

Ten handshapes

A, B, C, D, L, V, W, Y, ILY, and STOP are available in one prototype.

6

Six-second confirmation

A correct handshape must stay continuously matched before it is confirmed.

♪

Audible countdown

Every remaining second has a short tone, followed by a distinct success sound.

P

Personal Practice

Pick one handshape directly without completing a full lesson sequence.

↻

Attempt tracking

When a matched pose breaks during the countdown, the attempt counter records the reset.
