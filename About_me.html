About Me

A Canada & China Multidisciplinary Visual Artist / Senior Landscape Engineer / Life-long Learner

Biography

I am located in Toronto, Ca a multidisciplinary digital media artist and creative thinker working across graphic design, photography, video production, and 3D digital media. With years of experience as a designer and director, I've developed a strong visual sensibility and a practical understanding of how digital media communicate emotion, story, and brand identity across different platforms.

With a deep interest in the intersection of technology and creativity, my recent focus has shifted toward the role of AI in transforming media production and communication. In recent years within the AI-driven digital media field, I have consistently produced AI-based creative photography over a three-year period. Through the effective use of AI, I established an independent personal studio that would traditionally require the efforts of multiple individuals, while maintaining strong creative output. These accomplishments reflect my identity as a lifelong learner with a strong passion for knowledge and exploration.

I bring strong project management, time management, and coordination skills to my practice. I work efficiently under deadlines and collaborate effectively within team-based projects, contributing both creatively and organizationally to ensure high-quality outcomes.

My interdisciplinary background in art, science, and design shapes an experimental approach to digital media, expanding boundaries in both form and concept.
